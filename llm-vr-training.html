LLM-Assisted VR Training for Manufacturing

A modular, LLM-driven framework that turns unstructured standard operating procedures (SOPs) and spoken user questions into structured, executable VR training steps — validated across three real industrial case studies and published as first-author research.

First-author publication Unreal Engine 5 GPT-4o-mini / Gemini 2.5 Flash Whisper ASR RAG Meta Quest Pro

Overview

Traditional VR training is hand-authored: every step, prompt, and check is scripted by a developer, which makes it slow to build and hard to update when a procedure changes. This project replaces that manual authoring with an LLM-driven pipeline that reads a plant's existing documentation and generates the training on demand.

The system takes a factory's SOPs and manuals, lets a trainee ask for a task by voice, and produces a step-by-step VR procedure grounded in the real virtual equipment — while continuously monitoring the trainee's actions to keep them on track. It was built end-to-end in Unreal Engine and deployed on Meta Quest Pro.

Impact

  • Faster to build, easier to maintain. Training procedures are generated from existing SOPs and manuals instead of being hand-scripted, so new tasks and updates no longer require rebuilding the VR content.
  • Validated on real tasks. Tested across three industrial case studies — press-brake gage alignment, atomic layer deposition, and semiconductor wafer resistivity measurement — reaching up to 0.92 F1-score on procedural step alignment against expert reference SOPs.
  • Efficient at runtime. Pre-generated execution plans reduced live LLM calls, latency, and token cost, keeping the experience responsive in VR.

Novelty

  • Manager–worker LLM orchestration. A manager model routes each request across three intent classes (procedure generation, general Q&A, widget interaction) to specialized workers (GPT-4o-mini, Gemini 2.5 Flash), rather than relying on a single prompt.
  • Hallucination-guarded generation. A RAG pipeline with JSON-schema-constrained output and provenance metadata keeps generated instructions tied to source documents — important for safety-critical training.
  • Eye-tracking spatial grounding. A sphere-of-influence spatial filter with a custom Unreal Blueprint class grounds instructions to the correct virtual component in cluttered scenes.
  • Deterministic runtime verification. A state-based monitoring engine checks each action (distance, gaze dwell, positional error, snap-to-place within 5 cm) for stable, reliable step progression.

Industry adaptability

The framework is designed to drop into a real plant's workflow rather than a lab demo. Because it builds training from a company's own SOPs and manuals, it can be re-pointed at a new machine, line, or facility without re-authoring the experience by hand.

  • Works from documentation manufacturers already have, lowering the cost of standing up new training.
  • Voice-driven and hands-free, suited to shop-floor and assembly settings.
  • Developed with industry partners (Siemens Digital Industries, Cincinnati Inc., Valvoline) toward pilot deployment.
