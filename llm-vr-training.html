LLM-Assisted VR Training for Manufacturing

A modular, LLM-driven framework that turns unstructured standard operating procedures (SOPs) into scalable, executable VR training procedures — without hard-coding each step. Validated across real industrial case studies and published as first-author research.

First-author publication Unreal Engine 5 GPT-4o-mini / Gemini 2.5 Flash Whisper ASR RAG Meta Quest Pro

Objective

Develop an LLM-driven framework that turns unstructured SOPs into scalable, executable VR training procedures for manufacturing scenarios — without hard-coding each step. Traditional VR training is hand-authored, which makes it slow to build and hard to update when a procedure changes; this framework generates the training on demand from a plant's own documentation.

ROLE
First author · framework design and implementation
PARTNERS
Siemens Digital Industries · Cincinnati Inc. · Valvoline
STACK
Unreal Engine 5 (Blueprint + C++), GPT-4o-mini, Gemini 2.5 Flash, Whisper, RAG (all-MiniLM-L6-v2)
PUBLICATION
ASME IDETC-CIE 2026 (accepted; camera-ready submitted)

Impact

  • Reduces dependence on manually scripted workflows. Training is generated from existing SOPs and manuals instead of being hand-authored, so new tasks and updates no longer mean rebuilding the VR content.
  • Enables scalable operator training across diverse manufacturing machines and tasks.
  • Bridges unstructured SOPs and executable VR guidance — turning documents people already have into step-by-step procedures inside the headset.
  • Validated on real tasks. Across three case studies — press-brake gage alignment, atomic layer deposition, and wafer resistivity measurement — reaching up to 0.92 F1-score on procedural step alignment against expert reference SOPs.

Novelty

  • LLM abstraction layer. Turns unstructured SOPs into executable VR steps — a manager model routes each request (procedure generation, Q&A, widget interaction) to specialized workers (GPT-4o-mini, Gemini 2.5 Flash).
  • Pre-generated execution plans that cut latency, cost, and procedural drift, keeping the experience responsive in VR.
  • Unified grounding + monitoring. Eye-tracking with sphere-of-influence spatial filtering and action-driven verification keep instructions tied to the right component and confirm each step before advancing.
  • Hallucination-guarded generation. A RAG pipeline with JSON-schema-constrained output ties instructions to source documents — important for safety-critical training.

Industry adaptability

The framework is built to drop into a real plant's workflow, not just a lab demo. It runs as a simple pipeline over documentation manufacturers already have:

INPUT
Machine manuals & SOPs (PDFs), equipment list, 3D CAD model, and the VR factory / cleanroom environment.
PROCESSING
Extract and structure text, identify components from the equipment list, classify action keywords, generate procedure steps, and monitor user actions in VR.
OUTPUT
A VR training module with LLM-generated step-by-step instructions, real-time monitoring, and interactive VR HMI guidance.
USE CASES
Press-brake alignment procedure · semiconductor chip manufacturing.
  • Enables rapid reconfiguration of training when processes, machines, or safety standards change.
  • Reduces manual procedure-authoring effort while providing consistent, structured instructions.
