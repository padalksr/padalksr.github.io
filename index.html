About

Who I am and what I focus on.

I’m a mechanical engineer specializing in LLM-assisted VR/AR training systems for manufacturing and semiconductor industries, working at the intersection of CAD, AI, and immersive environments to make complex technical training more intuitive and accessible.

At the University of Cincinnati’s Smart Manufacturing Lab, I develop VR modules that integrate LLaMA-based assistants, eye tracking, and dynamic avatars to guide users step-by-step through semiconductor and mechanical processes.

My work bridges machine design and human–machine interaction, with the long-term goal of building intelligent training systems that scale to real factories and real workers.

Projects

Selected work that combines CAD, AI models, and immersive VR.

VR · LLM

LLM-Assisted VR Training Framework

A VR framework that uses LLaMA-based models to generate task instructions, answer user questions, and monitor progress inside a virtual cleanroom and manufacturing environment.

  • Integrates LLaMA 3.1, Whisper, and eye tracking in Unreal Engine.
  • Structured JSON outputs for actions, steps, and component-level monitoring.
  • Real-time latency analysis for VR deployment.
Unreal EngineLLaMA 3.1Whisper
Avatar · Ergonomics

Dynamic Avatar for Ergonomic Guidance

Real-time avatar system that demonstrates tasks in VR while embedding ergonomic principles to reduce risk and improve posture during training. (On-going)

  • Inverse kinematics and body calibration using motion-capture data.
  • Avatar actions aligned with ergonomic standards such as ISO 11226.
  • Designed for future user-specific ergonomic scoring.
IKMoCapErgonomics
Semiconductor · Education

Semiconductor Chip Manufacturing VR Module

An immersive VR module for handling cleanroom equipment and teaching cleanroom workflows for semiconductor chip manufacturing — without expensive equipment.

  • Step-by-step guided training with embedded videos and a feedback system.
  • Deployed in Intel-supported programs at UC and other OASiS partners.
  • Used by 60+ high-school students and 9 educators in Summer 2024 workshops.
